💡 Health Tips for a Healthier Heart

Learn more ↓
🍎
Choose heart-friendly foods

Eat more vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans and nuts. Limit excess salt and highly processed foods.

👟
Move a little every day

Start with a comfortable walk and build toward regular activity suited to your health and abilities.

🚭
Avoid tobacco

Not smoking protects your heart and blood vessels. Support is available if quitting feels difficult.

😴
Protect your sleep

Keep a regular sleep schedule and aim for enough restful sleep for your age and needs.

🧘
Make room to unwind

Try slow breathing, a relaxing hobby, or time with people who support you.
